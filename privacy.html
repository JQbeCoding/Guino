Guino

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 29, 2026

Overview

Guino is a personal academic dashboard that connects to your Canvas LMS account to show assignments, grades, and AI-generated study helpers.

Data We Use

  • Canvas data: course names, assignments, due dates, grades, and syllabus text fetched via your API token stored in server environment variables.
  • Settings: display name, theme, notification preferences, ntfy topic, and optional phone number stored in local settings.json on the server.
  • AI: assignment descriptions sent to Google Gemini to generate study plans and flashcards.
  • Notifications: ntfy topic or phone number you provide for opt-in reminders.

What We Do Not Do

Guino does not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for marketing.

Third-Party Services

Canvas (Instructure), Google Gemini, ntfy.sh, and optionally Twilio for SMS.

Your Choices

You can disable notifications anytime in Settings.
